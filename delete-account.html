🗑️ Delete Your SplitDown Account

We're sorry to see you go. You can request deletion of your account and all associated data by following the steps below.

⚠️ Warning: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. All your groups, expenses, and data will be permanently deleted.

What Gets Deleted

  • ✅ Your account and profile information
  • ✅ Groups you created
  • ✅ Expenses you added
  • ✅ Receipt photos you uploaded
  • ✅ Your UPI ID and phone number
  • ✅ All personal data associated with your account

What May Be Retained

  • ⚠️ Expense records in shared groups (visible to other group members) — retained for 30 days then deleted
  • ⚠️ Anonymous usage analytics (no personal information)

How to Delete Your Account

Option 1 — Email Request (Recommended):
  1. Send an email to fogan.studio@gmail.com
  2. Subject: "Delete My Account"
  3. Include your registered email address
  4. We will process your request within 7 days
Option 2 — In-App (Coming Soon):

We are working on adding an in-app account deletion feature in a future update.

Retention Period

After receiving your deletion request:

  • Account deleted immediately
  • Personal data purged within 7 days
  • Backup data purged within 30 days
