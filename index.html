SplitDown Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026

Introduction

SplitDown ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

Data We Collect

Information you provide:

  • Name and email address (for account creation)
  • UPI ID (optional, for payment convenience)
  • Phone number (optional, for UPI payments)
  • Group names, expense titles, and amounts
  • Receipt photos (optional)

Automatically collected:

  • Device information and OS version
  • App usage data and crash reports
  • Advertising ID (for ads)

How We Use Your Data

  • To provide bill splitting and expense tracking features
  • To sync your data across devices in real-time
  • To show relevant advertisements via Google AdMob
  • To improve app performance and fix bugs
  • To send settlement reminders to group members

Data Storage

Your data is securely stored on Google Firebase servers (asia-south1 region — Mumbai, India). We use industry-standard encryption to protect your data.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Firebase — Authentication, database, and storage
  • Google Sign-In — Authentication
  • Google AdMob — Advertisements
  • UPI Apps — Payment processing (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, etc.)

Data Sharing

We do NOT sell your personal data. We only share data with:

  • Service providers listed above (Firebase, AdMob)
  • Other group members (only group-related expense data)
  • Law enforcement if legally required

Data Retention

We retain your data as long as your account is active. You can delete your account and data at any time by contacting us.

Children's Privacy

SplitDown is not intended for users under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt out of personalized ads (in device settings)
  • Export your data

Permissions We Use

  • Camera & Storage — To attach receipt photos
  • Internet — To sync data across devices

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. We will notify you of significant changes through the app. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
